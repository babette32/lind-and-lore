Resources for Schools & Families

Adventure first, with space for compassion and conversation.

Babou’s Kingdom offers a story-led way to talk with children about war, forced displacement, migration, courage, welcome, and hope—without beginning with statistics or news footage.

The free Classroom & Family Pack is in development for readers aged 8–12. It will help teachers, librarians, parents, and carers explore the story through five connected themes.

  1. 01HomeWhat makes somewhere feel like home?
  2. 02LeavingWhy might a family have no choice but to leave?
  3. 03JourneyHow do courage, kindness, and friendship help?
  4. 04BelongingWhat helps someone new feel welcome?
  5. 05HopeHow do people imagine and build a future?

Coming in the free pack

Conversation prompts and creative activities

Planned resources include What Would You Take?, My Home Map, and The Welcome Kit, alongside discussion questions and gentle guidance for adults.
